## Multiroom audio with Music Assistant (Sendspin)

LibreEcho can now join [Music Assistant](https://music-assistant.io/) as a
Sendspin speaker, on its own or in a group of speakers that play in time with
each other. It is off by default: turn it on under **Integrations →
Sendspin (Music Assistant)**, then pair the speaker in Music Assistant with the
token shown on that card. See [the Sendspin guide](../audio/sendspin.md).

- Discovered automatically on the local network (mDNS); paired once with a
  token; the pairing survives restarts. **Forget paired servers** issues a new
  token and makes every server pair again.
- FLAC (preferred) or PCM, 48 kHz stereo. Opus is not supported.
- Volume is the speaker volume, in both directions. A mute from Music
  Assistant silences music only; alarms, timers and voice replies still play.
- Grouped speakers are aligned to their measured output delay, including each
  model's speaker processing.
- Playback starts about 1.5 seconds after pressing play while the amplifier
  powers up. A seek resumes in about 0.5 s, and a resume within a few seconds
  of pausing in under 1 s, because the speaker keeps its output running for
  5 seconds after music stops.

Validation (development builds, to be repeated on the release candidate):

- Echo (2nd generation) and Echo Dot grouped: acoustic skew 0.03-0.04 ms
  (two independent sessions); no drift over 30 minutes; no mid-stream
  dropouts in 30 minutes.
- Music Assistant 2.10.8: discovery, token pairing, grouped play, pause,
  resume, seek, leave and rejoin, volume and mute in both directions, FLAC.
- Server restart and stream clear recover on both speakers; an 8-member
  group (2 speakers + 6 virtual players) stays clean.
- Seek resume 1.66 s -> 0.51 s; Music Assistant pause/resume after 2 s
  ~0.85 s (was ~2.4 s, which a resume after a longer pause still takes).

Known limits: one Music Assistant server at a time. Rarely, a speaker that
starts or rejoins a group begins up to about 3 ms off and settles within a few
seconds.
